# Chicken Swaharma

https://youtu.be/F8PGWLvn1mQ?si=vj2ViWZUGDcu7EuW

# Ingredients

## Shawarma Style Seasoning
- 2 Tbsp paprika powder
- 1 Tbsp dried oregano
- 1/2 Tbsp jeera
- 1/2 Tbsp garlic powder
- 1/2 tsp cinnamon
- 1/2 Tbsp salt
- 1 tsp msg

## Shawarma Chicken & Veggie Tray Bake
- 6 large chicken drumsticks
- 6 tsp shawarma seasoning (or to taste)
- olive oil 
- 2 medium potatoes
- 1 small eggplant
1 small red bell pepper
2 Tbsp olive oil or cooking oil salt to taste, about 1/2 tsp add to tray
bake 30-35 mins @ 230C
soy sauce or maggi for drizzling after baking
Shawarma Sandwich Salad
1/2 cucumber, deseeded and diced
1 good large tomato, diced, drained
1 red onion, diced
about 1 cup or 6 Lebanese pickled cucumbers, diced
a dood handfu of nars ev leaves fine v chopped


## Also
- 1 medium head of iceberg lettuce
- 4 flatbread

## Recommended sauces:
- hummus
- Sriracha 
- mango chutney

# Recipe 

1. Place chicken drumsticks in a bowl and cover with seasoning and olive oil. Rub thoroughly. Place chicken on a sheet pan.

1. Using the same bowl, dice potatoes, eggplant and bell pepper. Mix with remains of seasoning. Place veggies on the same sheet pan evenly.

1. Bake 30-35 mins at 230°C

1. While baking, make the salad. Chop everything and combine in a (clean) bowl.

1. After baking, drizzle the pan sheet with soy sauce / maggi. Let it rest so you can handle the chicken.

1. On a cutting board, separate the chicken meat from the bone. Chop the meat coarsely. Mix back with veggies

1. Take a flatbread and add chicken, veggies, salad, lettuce, and sauces. Roll into a wrap & enjoy.
